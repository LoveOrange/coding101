隐私与 Cookie

本说明适用于个人开发者 Linsama 运营的技术学习站 coding101.linsama.com 和项目展示站 linsama.com。下文的账号与学习记录说明适用于 Coding 101。其他项目、博客及外部网站的数据处理以各自说明为准。

文章、题目和参考答案免费开放，无需登录。本隐私说明页面不加载任何脚本，包括广告、Google Analytics 和广告同意弹窗。

访问网站时的数据

网站通过 Cloudflare 提供页面、图片和安全防护。处理请求时，服务提供方会接收 IP 地址、请求网址、浏览器类型等连接信息，用于传输内容、防止滥用和排查故障。详情见 Cloudflare 隐私政策。

浏览器可能在本地保存主题和阅读界面偏好。你可以在浏览器的网站数据设置中清除这些记录；清除后相关偏好也会重置。本站当前公开的文章和面试自测不要求提交姓名、电话或支付资料。

账号与学习记录

Coding 101 使用邮箱验证码登录，无需设置密码。使用账号服务时，本站收集和保存以下数据：

  • 账号信息：邮箱地址、账号标识、邮箱验证状态和账号创建、更新时间，用于识别账号和发送登录验证码。
  • 登录会话：通过仅供 coding101.linsama.com 使用的会话 Cookie 保持登录。该 Cookie 设置为 HttpOnly，页面脚本无法读取。服务端记录登录时的 IP 地址和浏览器 User-Agent，用于安全与防滥用。
  • 学习记录：包括卡片复习状态、阅读进度、学习会话、偏好和模拟测试设置，单个账号的学习记录上限为 1 MB。
  • 试用与激活码兑换记录：保存权益起止时间、激活码兑换时间、天数、带密钥哈希值和掩码（首尾部分字符），不保存完整激活码明文。还会记录兑换尝试的时间、结果和 IP 地址的带密钥哈希值，用于限制反复猜码。

学习记录也会缓存在当前浏览器中，供页面读取并与服务端同步。退出登录或删除账号成功后，本站会清除当前浏览器的学习缓存；其他设备上的本地副本可以通过浏览器的网站数据设置清除。

账号数据的用途

上述账号数据仅用于登录、保存和同步学习记录、判断访问权限及防止滥用。本站不出售个人信息，也不使用这些数据发送营销邮件；登录验证码和账户服务通知属于服务所需邮件。

账号服务提供方

  • Cloudflare：提供托管、内容分发网络（CDN）、防滥用和请求日志服务；通过 Cloudflare Email Service 的 Email Sending 发送登录验证码，通过 Email Routing 转发发往联系邮箱的邮件。详情见 Cloudflare 隐私政策。
  • Neon：提供数据库服务，本站数据库位于新加坡区域 AWS ap-southeast-1。详情见 Neon 隐私政策。

保存期限

账号及关联的权益和兑换尝试记录保留到账号删除。Pro 或试用权益到期后，学习记录进入 90 天只读期；期满后由每日清理任务删除服务端学习记录，账号本身保留。只读期结束前重新开通 Pro，可以继续使用已保存的学习记录。

登录会话在退出登录或过期时失效。

  • 请求日志：当前 Workers Paid 套餐的 Workers Logs / Observability 日志保留 7 天。这个期限指 Workers 日志，不代表 Cloudflare 所有服务的数据期限。来源：Workers Logs 保留期。
  • 邮件事件日志：Cloudflare Email Sending 和 Email Routing 的发送、路由事件日志保留 31 天，可包含发件人、收件人、主题和投递结果。这不代表收件箱中的邮件会在 31 天后删除。来源：Email Service 事件数据与保留期。
  • 发送邮件正文预览：本站已开启 Cloudflare Email preview，发送邮件的正文预览保留约 7 天。来源：Message preview。
  • 数据库恢复历史：当前 Neon Free 套餐的时间点恢复（PITR）窗口设为 6 小时，历史数据另受 1 GB 上限约束。当前生产分支没有额外的 Neon 快照或定时快照计划。来源：Neon History window；实际设置和快照状态见运维清单 A5 的核验记录。

账号删除后，用于防止重复试用的邮箱 HMAC 和已用激活码记录仍保留，目前不设自动到期时间；用途和关联范围见下一节。日志、邮件正文预览和数据库恢复历史按上述各自期限保留，不会随账号删除立即清除。

删除账号、查询与导出数据

登录后，你可以在“账户 → 数据与隐私”页面删除账号。删除成功后，账号、会话、权益记录、兑换尝试和学习记录会从业务数据库移除，剩余权益作废。若账号存在仍可扣费的订阅，需先停止续费或处理未结清的订阅状态，才能删除账号。

已兑换的激活码保持“已使用”状态，并保留兑换时间等码记录，但解除账号关联。如果账号用过试用或 Pro，本站还会保留规范化邮箱的带密钥哈希值（HMAC）及其记录时间，用于防止同一邮箱重复领取试用。HMAC 记录不保存邮箱明文，但服务端可以对再次提交的邮箱计算 HMAC 并比对，因此不将它视为匿名数据。比对时忽略大小写和加号后缀，并将 Gmail 地址中的点及 googlemail.com 别名作统一处理。

如需查询或导出账号数据和学习记录，请发送邮件至 privacy@linsama.com，由运营者 Linsama 本人处理，并在收到申请后 30 天内答复。为保护账号数据，处理前可能需要核验邮箱归属。目前没有自助导出功能。

访问统计

网站使用 Google Analytics 了解页面访问和设备概况，以判断哪些主题有用、哪些入口需要改进。统计服务可能使用 Cookie，并接收页面地址、来源、浏览器和设备信息。我们关闭 Google signals 和用于广告个性化的统计信号；需要同意的地区会按同意状态限制存储。

Google 对数据的处理见 Google 隐私政策和Google 如何使用合作伙伴网站的数据。你可以使用浏览器的 Cookie 管理功能，或 Google Analytics 停用工具控制统计。

Google 广告

Coding 101 计划使用 Google AdSense 支持网站维护，审核通过并完成设置后才会开放广告。广告位置会标为“广告”，实际是否展示取决于审核状态、地区、用户选择和广告供应。广告不影响免费阅读，也不代表本站对广告内容作出推荐。

Google 和其他第三方广告供应商可能使用 Cookie，根据你此前访问本站或其他网站的记录投放广告。Google 的广告 Cookie 使其及合作伙伴能够根据访问记录展示个性化广告；其他广告网络也可能设置自己的 Cookie。服务提供方及其用途可在适用的广告隐私选项中查看。

你可以在 Google 我的广告中心管理个性化广告，或通过 Digital Advertising Alliance 的退出工具管理部分第三方供应商的选择。关闭个性化广告不一定会停止所有广告。

同意与撤回

向欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士等适用地区的访客提供广告时，网站使用 Google 的同意管理消息。你可以选择同意、不同意或管理具体用途。之后可使用显示广告页面底部的“隐私和 Cookie 设置”重新调整选择；拒绝同意后仍可阅读本站内容。

如果你的浏览器没有显示这类消息，可使用浏览器设置和上述 Google 工具管理 Cookie 与广告偏好。不同浏览器、设备和清除网站数据后的选择可能需要分别设置。

Privacy and cookies — English

This notice covers coding101.linsama.com and linsama.com, operated by independent developer Linsama. The account and learning-record sections apply to Coding 101. Articles, questions and reference answers are free to read without logging in. This privacy page loads no scripts, including ads, analytics or consent messages. Other linked projects and websites have their own policies.

Data collected when visiting

Cloudflare delivers and protects the sites and receives connection data such as IP addresses, requested URLs and browser information to deliver content, prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems. Browser storage may retain interface preferences; clearing site data resets them. Reading public articles and self-test questions does not require a name, phone number or payment details.

Account and learning records

Coding 101 uses email verification codes for passwordless login. When you use account services, we collect and store:

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, email verification status, and account creation and update times, used to identify your account and send login verification codes.
  • Login sessions: a host-only session cookie for coding101.linsama.com keeps you logged in. It is marked HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it. The server records your IP address and browser User-Agent at login for security and abuse prevention.
  • Learning records: card review states, reading progress, learning sessions, preferences and mock-test settings, with a limit of 1 MB per account.
  • Trial and activation-code redemption records: access start and end times, redemption time, number of days, a keyed hash of each code and a masked version showing some initial and final characters. We do not store complete activation codes in plain text. Redemption attempts also record the time, result and a keyed hash of the IP address to limit repeated guesses.

Learning records are also cached in the current browser for display and synchronization with the server. Signing out or successfully deleting your account clears this browser's learning cache. Local copies on other devices can be cleared through each browser's site-data settings.

How account data is used

We use this account data only to log you in, save and sync learning records, determine access rights and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal information or use this data for marketing emails. Login verification codes and account service notices are service-related emails.

Account service providers

  • Cloudflare: hosting, content delivery network (CDN), abuse prevention and request logs. Cloudflare Email Service sends login verification codes through Email Sending and forwards mail sent to contact addresses through Email Routing. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Neon: database services. Our database is hosted in the AWS ap-southeast-1 region in Singapore. See Neon's privacy policy.

Retention

Your account and its associated access entitlement and redemption-attempt records are retained until the account is deleted. After Pro or trial access expires, learning records remain read-only for 90 days. A daily cleanup task then deletes the server-side learning records; the account itself remains. Reactivating Pro before this period ends lets you continue using the saved learning records.

Login sessions become invalid when you log out or when they expire.

  • Request logs: Workers Logs / Observability logs on our current Workers Paid plan are retained for 7 days. This applies to Workers logs, not all Cloudflare services. Source: Workers Logs retention.
  • Email event logs: Cloudflare Email Sending and Email Routing event logs are retained for 31 days and may include sender, recipient, subject and delivery result. This does not mean messages in mailboxes are deleted after 31 days. Source: Email Service event data and retention.
  • Sent-message previews: Cloudflare Email preview is enabled for our sending domain. Sent-message body previews are retained for about 7 days. Source: Message preview.
  • Database recovery history: our current Neon Free plan has a 6-hour point-in-time recovery (PITR) window, also capped at 1 GB of history. The production branch currently has no additional Neon snapshots or snapshot schedule. Source: Neon History window; the actual settings and snapshot status are recorded in operations checklist A5.

After account deletion, the email HMAC used to prevent repeat trials and used activation-code records remain, with no automatic expiry currently set. Their purposes and account links are explained below. Logs, sent-message previews and database recovery history follow their respective retention periods above and are not immediately cleared by account deletion.

Account deletion, data access and export

After logging in, you can delete your account under Account → 数据与隐私 (Data and privacy). Successful deletion removes your account, sessions, access entitlement records, redemption attempts and learning records from the operational databases, and forfeits remaining access. If a subscription can still charge you, you must first stop renewal or resolve its outstanding status before deleting the account.

Redeemed activation codes remain marked as used, with code records such as the redemption time retained, but their account link is removed. If the account had used a trial or Pro, we also retain a keyed hash (HMAC) of the normalized email address and its record creation time to prevent repeat trials. The HMAC record does not store the plain-text address, but the server can compute and compare the HMAC of an address submitted again, so we do not treat it as anonymous data. Matching ignores letter case and plus suffixes, and also normalizes dots in Gmail addresses and the googlemail.com alias.

To request access to or an export of your account data and learning records, email privacy@linsama.com. Linsama, the site operator, handles these requests personally and will respond within 30 days of receiving your request. We may need to verify ownership of the email address before processing it. Self-service export is not currently available.

Turning off personalized ads does not necessarily stop all ads. Choices may need to be set separately across browsers and devices, or after clearing site data.
