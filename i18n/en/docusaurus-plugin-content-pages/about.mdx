# About Coding 101

Coding 101 is a technical interview preparation website built and maintained by [Linsama](https://linsama.com/resume/). Articles and self-assessment questions are free to read without an account. The site helps students, career changers and working engineers choose what to review and turn familiar concepts into answers they can explain independently.

## What you can study

English translations are being added; the [article collection](/articles/) lists those currently available. The full collection and learning tools remain available in Chinese. <ChineseLink to="/interview-roadmaps/">Role guides (Chinese)</ChineseLink> show the available paths and identify those still being built or planned.

Role guides distinguish review priority, reading order and answer depth. Technical articles explain mechanisms, examples and limits, followed by questions with collapsed reference answers. Try answering independently before opening them. Focus first on the essentials for your target role; add deeper topics when a job description, project or interview requires them.

## How the material is prepared

The material focuses on problems candidates need to explain or reason about in interviews. Technical claims are checked against official documentation, specifications, source code and original papers, with references provided in each article. Java material primarily covers versions 17 and 21; MySQL material uses 8.0. Claims that depend on a version state that scope.

The project provides explanations, code examples, boundary conditions and links between questions and the underlying concepts. Referenced courses, open-source projects, recruitment pages and third-party questions retain their sources and their authors' rights.

Questions marked as site-authored self-assessments are exercises, not verified company interview questions. Recruitment information and public interview accounts describe particular experiences at particular times. A company name does not imply a fixed question bank or an official partnership. The site does not guarantee employment outcomes; check current requirements with the employer.

## Maintenance and feedback

Document pages include their update time and a source editing link. If an explanation is wrong, an example does not work or a link is broken, use <ChineseLink to="/contact">Contact and corrections (Chinese)</ChineseLink> and include the page address and a specific description of the issue. Corrections update the relevant article directly.

The site plans to support maintenance with display advertising once approval and setup are complete. Ads are labelled separately from learning content and do not change reading access or imply endorsement. See [Privacy and cookies (Chinese)](https://coding101.linsama.com/privacy/) for data processing and choices.
